Privacy Policy

How BharatVerse protects your identity, cultural submissions, and personal information.

1. Commitment to Cultural Privacy

BharatVerse is a cultural digital preservation platform. We believe heritage belongs to the people. We collect minimal personal information solely to power your interactive journey, gamified XP achievements, and attribution for community folklore contributions.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Username, email address, chosen role (Explorer / Contributor), and encrypted passwords.
  • Cultural Contributions: Stories, oral folklore, traditional recipes, and media images you submit voluntarily to preserve living traditions.
  • Activity & Gamification: Completed quizzes, puzzle scores, journey milestones, and earned Cultural XP.

3. Community Submissions & Moral Rights

When you contribute an indigenous tradition, you retain moral credit as the recorder or contributor. We publish approved submissions under educational, non-commercial cultural heritage guidelines to ensure knowledge remains accessible for future generations.

4. Data Security & Third Parties

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to advertisers. Passwords are cryptographically hashed using PBKDF2 with SHA-256 encryption. All cloud data transmission is guarded via SSL/TLS encryption protocols.

5. Contact & Rights

You can edit your profile, request export of your submitted stories, or delete your account at any time by contacting the heritage curation committee at heritage@bharatverse.gov.in.

Last Updated: November 2026 • Compliant with Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act.
