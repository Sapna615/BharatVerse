BharatVerse AI Ethics & Guidelines

Our framework for responsible artificial intelligence in Indian cultural, spiritual, and historical heritage.

1. The Core Objective

The BharatVerse AI Assistant is built as an educational cultural companion, not a religious arbiter or political commentator. Its sole purpose is to illuminate India's art forms, classical languages, sacred architecture, and forgotten oral lore.

2. Civilisational Grounding & Anti-Hallucination

  • Verified Knowledge Retrieval: Our AI responses prioritize indexed knowledge records from classical literature, Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) records, Sangeet Natak Akademi archives, and verified community folklore.
  • Plurality of Perspectives: Where multiple regional recensions of an epic or festival exist (e.g., Valmiki Ramayana, Kamba Ramayanam, Ramcharitmanas), the AI highlights the richness of regional interpretations rather than imposing a single monolith.
  • Transparency: Whenever functioning in demo or retrieval mode, the AI explicitly cites sources and clarifies its reasoning boundary.

3. Safety & Hate Speech Mitigation

The AI is constrained from generating sectarian, divisive, blasphemous, or derogatory commentary. It treats all 22 official languages, indigenous tribal rituals, and cultural traditions with equal sanctity and scholarly detachment.

4. Human-in-the-Loop Oversight

AI suggestions do not overwrite living memory. All cultural submissions suggested by AI tools must pass human curator moderation before inclusion into national knowledge graphs.

Principles align with NITI Aayog's National Strategy for Artificial Intelligence (#AIforAll).
