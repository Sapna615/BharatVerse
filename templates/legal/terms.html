Respectful Digital Heritage

Terms of Heritage & Service

Guidelines ensuring authentic, respectful, and factual preservation of India's culture.

1. Purpose of BharatVerse

BharatVerse is a national student innovation platform (SIH26197) dedicated to discovering, digitizing, and safeguarding India's living cultural heritage. By accessing or registering on BharatVerse, you agree to uphold truthfulness, cultural dignity, and harmonious communal respect.

2. Code of Cultural Conduct

  • Respect Sacred Traditions: Users must not post disparaging, defamatory, blasphemous, or hateful remarks regarding any faith, tribe, caste, region, or indigenous practice.
  • Authenticity & Accuracy: Submissions must reflect genuine historical knowledge, living memories, or verified traditional folklore. Fabricated rumors or misinformation will be rejected during moderation.
  • No Commercial Exploitation: You may not scrape, sell, or commercialize traditional cultural knowledge or folk art patterns hosted on BharatVerse.

3. Curator Moderation & Review

Community submissions do not appear publicly until reviewed by our editorial curators. Curators reserve the right to edit descriptions for clarity, request revisions, or decline submissions that do not meet verification standards.

4. Educational Use & Attribution

All texts, digital library excerpts, and interactive puzzles are made accessible for academic, student, and cultural appreciation purposes under fair use doctrine.

Effective Date: November 2026 • Developed under Smart India Hackathon initiative.
